One line, at most 200 characters, roasting a smart fridge — its touchscreen now shows ads for yogurt.

> My fridge's touchscreen now shows ads for yogurt. I bought a fridge, not a billboard that keeps my food cold as a side job.

`main.ts` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`.
